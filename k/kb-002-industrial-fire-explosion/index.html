About

An evidence-backed specialist system covering industrial fire and explosion hazards: flammable atmospheres, dust explosions, vapour cloud explosions, BLEVEs, ignition sources and control, hazardous area classification, and the UK and EU regimes that govern dangerous substances and major accident hazards. Answers are traceable to stored sources and are machine-checked for unsupported claims before they are returned.

In scope

  • flammable atmospheres and explosive atmospheres
  • dust explosions and combustible dust
  • vapour cloud explosion and BLEVE
  • flash point lower and upper explosive limits
  • autoignition and ignition sources
  • deflagration and detonation
  • hazardous area classification and zoning
  • explosion prevention and protection measures
  • major accident hazard regulation
  • industrial fire and explosion incident precedent

Explicitly out of scope

  • medical diagnosis treatment or clinical advice
  • legal advice or litigation strategy
  • tax accounting and financial advice
  • share prices and investment advice
  • military ordnance and weapon design
  • domestic cooking fire advice
  • political opinion and elections

Standing caveat. This is an evidence-retrieval system, not a basis-of-design authority and not a substitute for a DSEAR or COMAH assessment by a competent person. Verify every figure against the cited primary source.

Known limitations

  • This knowledge area is a seed built to prove the factory is repeatable. Its corpus is much smaller than Knowledge Area #001's and leans heavily on tertiary reference sources; treat its coverage as indicative, not complete.
  • ATEX, DSEAR and COMAH primary legal texts and the NFPA and IEC standards in this field are not fully held. Where a standard governs a topic the system can point at it but cannot quote it.
  • Flash points, explosive limits and autoignition temperatures are strongly dependent on substance, pressure, temperature and test method. Any single figure should be read as specific to the conditions its source reports.

Cited but not held

These are copyrighted standards. The system can tell you they are relevant and point at them. It cannot quote their clauses, and it will decline if you ask it to.
