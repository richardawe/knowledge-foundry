About

An evidence-backed specialist system covering lithium-ion cell and system failure: failure mechanisms, thermal runaway initiation and propagation, vent-gas composition, early detection, suppression and cooling, battery energy storage and electric-vehicle incident precedent, and the transport, storage, installation and waste rules that govern them. Every answer is traceable to a stored source with a URI, publisher and retrieval date, and is machine-checked for unsupported claims before it is returned.

In scope

  • lithium-ion cell and battery failure mechanisms
  • thermal runaway onset initiation and propagation
  • internal and external short circuit
  • lithium plating and dendrite growth
  • separator failure and shutdown behaviour
  • vent gas composition and off-gassing
  • early detection of cell failure
  • fire suppression and cooling of battery fires
  • battery energy storage system fire safety
  • electric vehicle and e-bike battery fires
  • battery transport storage and installation rules
  • waste battery handling and treatment
  • battery safety standards and regulation
  • battery incident and product safety reports

Explicitly out of scope

  • medical diagnosis treatment or clinical advice
  • legal advice or litigation strategy
  • tax accounting and financial advice
  • share prices company valuation and investment advice
  • battery manufacturing cost economics and pricing forecasts
  • political opinion and elections
  • software programming help unrelated to batteries
  • personal relationship advice

Standing caveat. This is an evidence-retrieval system, not a basis-of-design authority. It does not replace a competent fire engineer, a dangerous-goods adviser or the governing standard. Verify every figure against the cited primary source before relying on it for a safety decision.

Known limitations

  • The full texts of NFPA 855, UL 9540A, IEC 62619, UN 38.3 and UNECE R100 are copyrighted and are NOT stored. They are registered as pointers only: the system can tell you a standard exists and is relevant, and cite secondary sources that describe it, but it cannot quote its clauses.
  • Thermal-runaway onset temperatures, vent-gas composition and propagation behaviour vary substantially with cell chemistry, format, state of charge and ageing. Any single figure should be read as specific to the test conditions of the source that reports it.
  • Academic coverage is drawn from open-access preprints. It is neither a systematic review nor peer-reviewed at the point of ingestion, and recent preprints may be superseded.
  • Coverage is strongest for UK and EU regulation and weakest for US state fire codes, Chinese GB standards and Japanese regulation.
  • Encyclopaedia sources (authority 2) are included for coverage and vocabulary. They are deliberately outranked by regulators and investigation bodies, but they are still cited where nothing better exists.
