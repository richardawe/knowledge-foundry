Summary — chars 1028–1511

Product: Windgoo B20 Electric Bike

Hazard: The product presents a risk of fire as it has been identified that when the electric bike is being charged, it is possible for the lithium-ion batteries to go into failure mode, potentially leading to a thermal runaway and fire.

Corrective action: Removal of the listing by the online marketplace (eBay)

We strongly recommend that owners stop using this product immediately. Contact the distributor you purchased from to request redress.

Hazard — chars 1791–2419

The product presents a risk of fire as it has been identified that when the electric bike is being charged, it is possible for the lithium-ion batteries to go into failure mode, potentially leading to a thermal runaway and fire.

Additionally, the responsible person has been unable to supply documentation to the satisfaction of the National Regulator that it meets the essential safety requirements. The instructions provided with the product were deemed insufficient as they did not contain all the required safety information.

The product does not meet the requirements of The Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008.

Corrective action — chars 2443–2634

Removal of the listing by the online marketplace (eBay).

We strongly recommend that owners stop using this product immediately. Contact the distributor you purchased from to request redress.
