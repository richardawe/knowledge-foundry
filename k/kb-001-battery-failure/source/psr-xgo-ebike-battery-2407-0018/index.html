Summary — chars 1084–1325

Product: X-Go E-Bike Lithium-Ion Battery (Bullet)

Hazard: The product presents a high risk of fire as the internal componentry of the battery is poorly connected.

Corrective action: The import has been rejected at the border and destroyed.

Product Report and ImageLink to product image and PDF

Hazard — chars 1628–2068

The product presents a high risk of fire as the internal componentry of the battery was poorly connected, with wires trapped and solder joints directly on wires with removed sheathing. Under fault conditions, this could cause the product to enter into thermal runaway, causing a fire. Improvements are also required to marking and labelling.

The product does not meet the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

Corrective action — chars 2092–2149

The import has been rejected at the border and destroyed.
